FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is DSS?
        Dimension Sorting Society is a closed organization responsible for sorting and cataloguing anomalous people, objects, and events across alternate Dimensions. This organization is also responsible for keeping aforementioned anomalies in check or in dire situations, removed.

Alternate Dimensions are real?
        Yes, Alternate Dimensions are real.

Is this site an elaborate practical joke?
        No, this site is not a practical joke. This is Dimension Sorting Society's one sole website intended for agents to use.

Am I going insane?
        No one is going insane. A website cannot render anyone insane. If you feel like you are, you probably have accessed the wrong DSS website. However, if you are sure that you accessed the right website and YOU ARE actively going insane, book an immediate consultation with Dr. Magnus Booker. (Yes, the pun was intended.)
